Dicey Baseball

Roll the dice. Play ball.

Every at-bat starts with a roll.

Two dice.
Nine innings.

Add up the two dice and the chart decides: a single, a double, a home run - or a strikeout. Roll doubles for a Hot Bat and roll again, keeping the better result. Snake eyes is a home run and a bonus Timeout. Rolled an out? Spend a Timeout to re-roll it.

You call the pitch.

Outsmart the computer.

Throw a fastball, issue an intentional walk to set up a double play, or bring the infield in to cut off a run at the plate. Play Rookie, Pro or All-Star - or hand the phone to a friend for pass-and-play. Down a run in the last inning? Walk it off.

How do I restore Pro on a new phone?

Open the game, tap ⭐️ Pro on the main screen, then tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it. Dicey Golf Pro and Dicey Baseball Pro are separate purchases.

How do I unlock things without paying?

Just play. In Dicey Golf, finishing a course unlocks the next one, and finishing under par unlocks its Night Golf version. In Dicey Baseball, winning unlocks new stadiums and night games. In both games, every achievement earns a new ball (or bat).

What are Timeouts in Dicey Baseball?

Each game you get 3 Timeouts. When you roll an out, tap Timeout to re-roll it. Rolling snake eyes (1-1) earns a bonus Timeout, and you can watch an optional ad for an extra one.

How do I turn off personalized ads?

Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking on your iPhone and turn tracking off for the game. Ads will still appear, but they won't be personalized. Pro removes the ads between holes and innings.

I closed the app in the middle of a game. Is it lost?

No - Dicey Golf saves after every shot and Dicey Baseball after every at-bat. Tap Resume on the main screen to pick up where you left off.

How do I reset my progress?

Tap the ⚙︎ gear on the main screen, then Reset all progress and tap it again to confirm. This erases achievements, collectibles, unlocks, stats and any saved game on that device.
