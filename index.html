How do I restore Dicey Golf Pro on a new phone?

Open Dicey Golf, tap ⭐️ Pro on the main screen, then tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it.

How do I unlock courses and night golf without paying?

Just play. Finishing a course unlocks the next one, finishing any course unlocks Random Links, and finishing a course under par unlocks its Night Golf version. Every achievement earns a new golf ball.

How do I turn off personalized ads?

Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking on your iPhone and turn tracking off for Dicey Golf. Ads will still appear, but they won't be personalized. Dicey Golf Pro removes the ads between holes.

I closed the app in the middle of a round. Is it lost?

No - your round saves after every shot. Tap Resume on the main screen to pick up where you left off.

How do I reset my progress?

Tap the ⚙︎ gear on the main screen, then Reset all progress and tap it again to confirm. This erases achievements, balls, unlocks, stats and best scores on that device.
