Privacy Policy

Dicey Golf · Last updated September 30, 2026

The short version: I don't collect your personal information. There are no accounts, and your game progress stays on your phone. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device data to show and measure those ads.

Who makes Dicey Golf

Dicey Golf is made by Johnathon Nehls ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information the Dicey Golf app uses and why.

Information stored on your device

Your scores, stats, unlocked courses, achievements, chosen golf ball, player names, settings and any saved round are stored only on your device. They are not sent to me. Deleting the app deletes this information.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Dicey Golf uses Google AdMob to show ads, including optional ads you can watch for a bonus mulligan. To show, limit and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • device identifiers, such as the advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when the app asks
  • your approximate location, based on your IP address
  • information about the ads you see and interact with
  • diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data

If you choose "Ask App Not to Track," ads are still shown, but they are not personalized using the advertising identifier. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Google's use of this data is covered by its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In-app purchases

The optional Dicey Golf Pro purchase is processed entirely by Apple. I never receive your name, payment details or Apple ID. Apple only tells the app whether Pro has been purchased so it can be unlocked and restored.

No accounts and no selling of data

Dicey Golf has no sign-up or accounts. I do not sell, rent or share personal information. Apart from the AdMob data described above, the app does not send information off your device.

Children

Dicey Golf is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact me and I will help address it.

Your choices

  • Turn off ad tracking: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking
  • Remove the ads between holes with Dicey Golf Pro
  • Erase all game data: Dicey Golf > Settings > Reset all progress, or delete the app

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
